AnnotatedText

com.alecdorrington.annotatedtextformat.AnnotatedText
See theAnnotatedText companion object
final case class AnnotatedText[+A]

A text with annotations, each attaching a value to some of its spans. Annotations may overlap and nest one another, but each has at least one span, and its own spans lie within the text, in order, none overlapping another.

Type parameters

A

The type of the annotations' values, specific to the application, such as a correction or a translation.

Value parameters

annotations

Its annotations.

text

The text annotated.
