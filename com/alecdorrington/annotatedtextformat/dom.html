The annotations that focusing gives take the focus, as by Tab, at the first of their elements that holds any text, showing the tooltip for its first line, which follows the line as the page moves: Tab moves the focus on into the tooltip, if anything there takes it, and on from its last part back to the element, and so on beyond it; Shift and Tab moves from its first part back to the element; and Escape closes it, giving the focus back to the element. Hidden annotations take the focus too, but show nothing.

final class Passage

A stretch of a page, beneath root: its text nodes, and the elements within it that are not prose, as code, each of which stands in its text as one Passage.placeholder, so that the text on either side of it is not taken as side by side. An element that a decoration put there stands as the text it shows, as a translation, so that a page decorated a piece at a time reads as it is seen, though nothing within it may be annotated again. Each <br>, and each boundary between blocks within it, as paragraphs, stands in its text as a line break. Annotations of the passage are of its text.

final class Tooltip

A tooltip in a page's top layer, where no part of the page can clip or cover it, holding any content in a shadow root, which the page's styles do not reach. It is kept within the window, and content too large for it scrolls within it. If it is interactive, it covers the gap between it and the line it is for as well, though it shows nothing there, so that the pointer crossing to it is over it all the way, and never over what lies beneath the gap, as a highlight on the next line, which would show a tooltip of its own. Each time it is shown is a Tooltip.Showing of its own, by which its content may be replaced while it lasts, as by an entry that arrives later, and it is placed again as its size changes.

A tooltip in a page's top layer, where no part of the page can clip or cover it, holding any content in a shadow root, which the page's styles do not reach. It is kept within the window, and content too large for it scrolls within it. If it is interactive, it covers the gap between it and the line it is for as well, though it shows nothing there, so that the pointer crossing to it is over it all the way, and never over what lies beneath the gap, as a highlight on the next line, which would show a tooltip of its own. Each time it is shown is a Tooltip.Showing of its own, by which its content may be replaced while it lasts, as by an entry that arrives later, and it is placed again as its size changes.

Its host is the element in the page, an atf-tooltip, which no rule of the page for its own elements matches, with an id of its own, by which what it is for is described, and the role of a dialog if it is interactive, or else of a tooltip. It takes the attribute data-side, above or below, as it stands of the line it is for. It goes at the root of the page, or in the modal dialog open, outside which all is inert.

Its content inherits nothing from the page, so its styles must set its font, its colours and its background.

The pointer's presses, clicks and moves within it, and keys typed in it, if it is interactive, go no further than it, so that the page does not act on them, and a press leaves the focus where it was, but in a field, so that its text cannot be selected by the pointer. The page's own listeners on its window or its document that capture events still hear of them first, as nothing within the page can stop them.
