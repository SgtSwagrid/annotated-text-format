def anchor[A](text: String, quoted: List[QuotedAnnotation[A]], matching: Matching = ...): Anchored[A]

quoted, anchored to text, as given in the order in which their passages begin. Each is placed by the first of these ways that finds it:

quoted, anchored to text, as given in the order in which their passages begin. Each is placed by the first of these ways that finds it:

  • where it first occurs from where the one before it began on, but not just where that one is, as a passage nested in it may begin where it does, but a word quoted again is a later occurrence;
  • else just where the one before it is, as two may annotate the same words;
  • else wherever it occurs only once, as a model may give one out of order, though where the search goes on from never moves back.

Within each way, the quote is sought as written before as loosely as matching allows, so that a correction of the case or spacing of a word lands on the word as written. Each part of a quote is found after the part before it, and a quote holding an ellipsis is first sought whole, as the text may hold one.

def quote[A](annotated: AnnotatedText[A], matching: Matching = ...): List[QuotedAnnotation[A]]

The annotations of annotated, in the order in which they begin, quoted as a model would write them, each with as few words around it as anchor it back to where it is, as anchor finds it: none, unless the same words stand earlier where they would be found instead, and then a word or more either side, the passage marked between them. Each point is quoted with the word on either side of it, or more.

The annotations of annotated, in the order in which they begin, quoted as a model would write them, each with as few words around it as anchor it back to where it is, as anchor finds it: none, unless the same words stand earlier where they would be found instead, and then a word or more either side, the passage marked between them. Each point is quoted with the word on either side of it, or more.

An annotation that no quote within reach places back, as in a text repeating the same words further than eight words either side, or one holding caret, open or close itself, is quoted with as many words around it as may be, and may not anchor back; anchor tells which do not, by where it places them.
