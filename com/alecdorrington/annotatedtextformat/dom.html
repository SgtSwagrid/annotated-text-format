final class Passage

A stretch of a page, beneath root: its text nodes, and the elements within it that are not prose, as code, or a decoration's own, each of which stands in its text as one Passage.placeholder, so that the text on either side of it is not taken as side by side. Each <br>, and each boundary between blocks within it, as paragraphs, stands in its text as a line break. Annotations of the passage are of its text.
